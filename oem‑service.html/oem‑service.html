OEM & ODM Custom Toy Service

From your sketch, image or brand IP to finished physical toys. Hanhang provides full‑cycle custom toy manufacturing for global commercial buyers.

Service Scope

  • ODM：Based on our existing toy styles, modify colors, logos and packaging for your brand
  • OEM：Full custom from your drawing, reference picture or brand mascot
  • Hand‑made prototype sampling & multiple revisions
  • Mold development for plastic toy items
  • Logo printing, embroidery, hang tag, private‑label box customization
  • Safety testing support & certification document supply

Our Custom Workflow

1. Requirement Brief

Share reference images, size, material, MOQ, target market safety standard and packaging requirements.

2. Quotation & Sample

We send quotation. Once confirmed, we produce prototype sample for your review.

3. Sample Revision & Confirm

Adjust appearance, structure and details until you approve the final sample.

4. Mass‑Production & QC

Start bulk production, implement in‑line quality inspection during production.

5. Test, Pack & Ship

Complete safety test, finish custom packaging and arrange shipment.
