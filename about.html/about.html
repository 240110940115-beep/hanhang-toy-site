About Hanhang

Hanhang is a one‑stop toy OEM & ODM manufacturer focusing on custom plush toys, promotional gifts and plastic toys for overseas brands, retailers and gift agencies. We combine design, sampling, mold development and mass‑production under one roof.

Our Strengths

  • Full‑chain service：Concept → Prototype → Mold → Bulk Production → Custom Packaging
  • Compliant with EN71, ASTM F963, CE toy safety standards, support third‑party lab testing
  • Flexible MOQ: support small trial orders and large‑volume wholesale
  • Private label, custom logo and exclusive packaging solution
  • Complete certification documents for EU & US customs clearance

Who We Work With

Global brand owners, Amazon sellers, marketing & gift companies, IP license holders, corporate merchandise projects.
