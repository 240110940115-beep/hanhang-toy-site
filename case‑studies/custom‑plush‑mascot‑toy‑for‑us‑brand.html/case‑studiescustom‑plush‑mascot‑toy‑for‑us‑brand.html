Custom Plush Mascot Toy For US Brand – Custom OEM Toy Manufacturing Project

Project Overview

  • Cooperation Client: US Local Brand Company
  • Toy Type: Plush / Custom Mascot
  • Service Provided: Custom Design, Prototype, Logo Printing, Private Label, Custom Packaging
  • Safety Standard: EN71, ASTM F963, CE Certified
  • Production Capacity: Bulk Mass Production Support

Custom Toy Project Solution & Process

This customized plush mascot toy project was tailored for US brand client, for retail sales and brand promotion. Hanhang provides one‑stop OEM & ODM toy manufacturing service from initial concept design, prototype sampling, mold development to bulk production and customized packaging.

1. Custom Requirement Confirmation

Confirmed toy size, material, custom logo, color scheme, packaging style and US market safety standards according to the client’s brand positioning and import requirements.

2. Prototype Sampling & Revision

Completed handmade sample production, adjusted details of appearance and structure, and confirmed the final design solution with the client before mass production.

3. Formal Bulk Production

Started standardized mass production in the factory, strictly controlled product quality, and implemented full‑process inspection to ensure all toys meet US safety standards.

4. Safety Testing & Delivery

All finished products passed EN71 and ASTM F963 safety tests, provided complete certification documents for customer customs clearance, and completed unified packaging and delivery.
